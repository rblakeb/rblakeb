# Content & Moderation Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This policy explains what isn't allowed on [SITE NAME], how we enforce it, how to report something, and how to appeal. It is part of our [Terms of Use](/terms).

## Zero tolerance

We permanently ban accounts and report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement for:
- any sexual content involving a minor, or anyone who appears to be a minor;
- anyone under 18 using the Service;
- showing or recording anyone without their consent;
- sexual content involving anyone who isn't verified, or who can't or doesn't consent.

## Not allowed anywhere
- Nudity or sexual activity outside the Adult 18+ room.
- Another person appearing on camera without their own verified account.
- Recording, screenshotting or restreaming other users.
- Offering or requesting payment or gifts for sexual activity.
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or promotion of violence or self-harm.
- Illegal activity, including drugs and weapons.
- Spam, advertising and links to other services.
- Getting around verification, moderation, blurring or bans.

## How we moderate
- **Age verification** with ID, a live selfie and a face match before anyone enters the Adult 18+ room. We may require it again at any time.
- **Calls go through our servers**, so a moderator can end any call immediately for both people.
- **Automated detection** blurs likely nudity in non-adult rooms and flags it for review. [Server-side frame analysis: describe once live.]
- **User reports.** Every call has a Report button. Accounts reported repeatedly are suspended automatically pending review.
- **Human review** of reports and flags by our moderation team. [Coverage hours / response target.]

## Reporting
- **In a call:** press Report. The call ends right away and the report reaches our team with the account involved.
- **Anything else**, including a complaint by someone who appeared in content without consent, or a request to remove content: email [SAFETY EMAIL] with what happened, when, and the display name if you know it.
- **Possible child exploitation:** report it in the app and to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org. If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

We review every report and aim to resolve complaints within [7 business days]. Reports involving minors or non-consensual content are prioritized and acted on as fast as possible.

## Removal requests from people who appeared on the Service
We don't record calls, so in most cases nothing is stored to remove. If any content of you is held by us, for example a flagged frame kept for review, and you didn't consent to appear, email [SAFETY EMAIL]. We'll remove it unless the law requires us to keep it, such as for an NCMEC report. If someone recorded you on another service, tell us, and we'll act against their account and help where we can.

## Appeals
If your account was restricted or banned and you believe it was a mistake, email [APPEALS EMAIL] within [30 days] with your display name. A person who wasn't involved in the original decision will review it, and we'll reply within [14 days]. Zero-tolerance bans are final when the evidence is clear.

## Records
We keep records of reports, moderation decisions and appeals as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), and review them regularly to improve our safety tools.
