# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This policy explains what [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") collects when you use [SITE NAME] (the "Service"), why, who we share it with, and your choices. The Service is only for people 18 and older.

## What we collect

**When you start using the Service**
- The display name you choose.
- A random session code saved in your browser so you stay signed in. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it.
- When you were last active.

**When you use rooms and calls**
- Which room you choose, and the language and interests you enter, used only to match you while you're waiting.
- Text chat messages, passed to the person you're matched with. We don't keep a copy.
- Audio and video, sent through our media servers to the person you're matched with. **We don't record calls.** Our moderation tools may analyze still frames automatically, for example to detect nudity in non-adult rooms. A flagged frame may be kept for review, for up to [30 days], or longer if needed for a legal report or investigation.
- Reports you make or receive, including the reason given.

**When you verify your age (Adult 18+ room)**
Verification is done by our provider, [VERIFICATION PROVIDER, e.g. Didit], which collects a photo of your ID and a live selfie and compares your face to the ID photo. **This involves biometric data. The provider processes it under its own privacy policy and only with your consent, given in the verification flow.** We receive and keep only:
- whether you passed, and whether you're 18 or older;
- the sex listed on your ID, used only to apply pricing;
- the provider's reference number for your check.

We don't receive or store your ID image, selfie, face data, legal name, date of birth or address.

**When you buy or refund credits**
Our payment processor, [Segpay], collects your payment details. We receive:
- your email address;
- a transaction reference;
- the pack you bought and the amount paid.

We keep a record of your credits, how they were used, and any refunds. We never receive your full card number.

**Automatically**
Our servers log technical information such as IP address, browser type, and the time and type of requests. We use it for security, fraud prevention and keeping the Service running, and keep it for [90 days].

**In your browser**
We save your session code and a few preferences (for example, that you dismissed the install tip) in your browser's local storage. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

## How we use it

- To run the Service: matching, calls, credits, refunds.
- To keep it safe: verification, moderation, reports, bans, and fraud and chargeback prevention.
- To contact you about your account, such as refund notices and inactivity reminders.
- To meet legal obligations: reporting child sexual abuse material to NCMEC, responding to lawful requests, and keeping financial records.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.
